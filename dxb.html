YOUR JOURNEY

Dubai, Day by Day.

Make the most of your holiday with this suggested five-day Dubai itinerary. Explore world-famous landmarks, enjoy a desert adventure and discover the city's modern attractions and traditional neighbourhoods. Activities can be adjusted to your travel dates, interests and budget.

DAY 01 / ARRIVAL
✈️

Welcome to Dubai

Arrive at Dubai International Airport and transfer to your hotel. Complete check-in and enjoy some leisure time. If your arrival schedule permits, explore nearby attractions or enjoy an evening stroll around the city.

Airport Transfer Hotel Check-in Leisure Time
DAY 02 / CITY TOUR
🏙️

Discover Modern Dubai

Explore Dubai's celebrated landmarks, including Burj Khalifa and Dubai Mall. Visit the Dubai Fountain area, admire the city's spectacular architecture and discover the lively Downtown district.

Burj Khalifa Dubai Mall Downtown Dubai
DAY 03 / DESERT ADVENTURE
🏜️

Desert Safari Experience

Head into the golden desert for an optional safari experience. Depending on the selected tour, enjoy dune driving, sunset photography, traditional entertainment and dinner at a desert camp.

Golden Dunes Sunset Views Desert Camp
DAY 04 / COAST & CULTURE
🌊

Dubai Marina & Old Dubai

Discover the waterfront scenery of Dubai Marina and the Palm Jumeirah area. Later, explore Old Dubai, visit traditional souks and experience the historic neighbourhoods around Dubai Creek.

Dubai Marina Palm Jumeirah Traditional Souks
DAY 05 / DEPARTURE
🌏

Farewell to Dubai

Enjoy breakfast and some free time before checking out. Depending on your flight schedule, visit a nearby shopping destination or pick up souvenirs before transferring to the airport for your return journey.

Breakfast Shopping Airport Transfer
