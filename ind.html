Discover India in 10 Days.

DAYS 01–02
🏛️

Delhi — Capital Highlights

Arrive in Delhi and explore selected highlights such as India Gate, Qutub Minar, Humayun's Tomb, the Red Fort area and the city's famous markets.

Heritage City Tour Local Cuisine

Agra — The Taj Mahal

Travel to Agra and visit the magnificent Taj Mahal and Agra Fort. Enjoy the city's Mughal heritage before continuing your journey towards Rajasthan.

Travel to the Himalayan region according to your selected transport arrangements. Enjoy Manali's mountain scenery, local markets, riverside landscapes and nearby attractions, subject to season and weather.
