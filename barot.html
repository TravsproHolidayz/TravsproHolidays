Bir & Barot Valley Escape

Begin your holiday in Bir with local sightseeing and leisure time. Optional paragliding can be requested separately.


On Day 2, continue towards Barot via the proposed Chehna Pass and Rajgundha route. Spend the night in Barot before departing for Joginder Nagar via Tikken, Jhatingri and Ghatasni on Day 3.


Accommodation, meals, transport and any guided sections will be specified in your final quotation.

Bir → Chehna Pass → Rajgundha → Barot → Joginder Nagar
YOUR DAY-BY-DAY JOURNEY

Three Days.
A Refreshing Escape.

One overnight stay in Bir and one in Barot. Final arrangements are subject to confirmation.

DAY01

Arrive In Bir & Enjoy Local Sightseeing

Arrival in Bir · Local sightseeing · Optional paragliding
  • Arrive in Bir and check in at your accommodation's scheduled check-in time.
  • Enjoy local sightseeing according to your arrival time and confirmed arrangements.
  • Spend some leisure time exploring the local surroundings.
  • Optional paragliding is available on request at an additional cost, subject to weather, operator availability and eligibility.
  • Return to your accommodation for the evening.
🏨 Overnight stay in Bir
DAY02

Bir To Barot Via Chehna Pass & Rajgundha

Bir → Chehna Pass → Rajgundha → Barot
  • Check out from your accommodation in Bir.
  • Begin your journey towards Barot via the proposed Chehna Pass and Rajgundha route.
  • Enjoy suitable scenic stops where access, conditions and the day's schedule permit.
  • Any walking, trekking, guide or transport arrangements must be confirmed before the trip.
  • Arrive in Barot and check in to your selected accommodation.
  • Spend the evening at leisure.
🏨 Overnight stay in Barot
DAY03

Barot To Joginder Nagar

Barot → Tikken → Jhatingri → Ghatasni → Joginder Nagar
  • Check out from your accommodation in Barot.
  • Depart towards Joginder Nagar via Tikken, Jhatingri and Ghatasni.
  • Enjoy suitable sightseeing or photo stops en route, depending on conditions and your onward travel schedule.
  • Arrive at your agreed drop-off point in Joginder Nagar.
  • Continue with your onward journey.
📍 Tour ends in Joginder Nagar
Important route note: The Day 2 route is proposed as requested. Local access, transport suitability and any walking or trekking sections must be confirmed before booking. This itinerary does not guarantee an entirely vehicle-based transfer via Chehna Pass and Rajgundha. Routes and activities may change due to weather, access restrictions and availability.

BEFORE YOU GO

A Few Helpful
Details.

Where does the tour start and finish?

The proposed tour starts in Bir on Day 1 and finishes in Joginder Nagar on Day 3. Exact pickup and drop-off points will be confirmed in your quotation.

Is paragliding included?

Paragliding is optional and costs extra unless explicitly included in your quotation. It is subject to weather, operator availability and participation requirements.

Is Day 2 entirely a vehicle journey?

This has not been confirmed. The proposed route via Chehna Pass and Rajgundha requires local verification. Any walking, trekking, guide or transport requirements must be agreed before booking.

Can I customise this holiday?

You can request additional nights, different accommodation or revised arrangements. Changes are subject to feasibility, availability and a revised quotation.

What are the payment and cancellation terms?

Request the applicable payment schedule, cancellation charges and refund conditions in writing before confirming your booking.
