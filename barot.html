Day-by-day itinerary

Your Bir & Barot Travel Plan.

A suggested 2-night itinerary with one overnight stay in Bir and one in Barot. Final arrangements are subject to confirmation.

DAY 01

Arrive in Bir & Enjoy Local Sightseeing

Arrival in Bir · Local sightseeing
  • Arrive in Bir and check in at your selected accommodation.
  • Enjoy local sightseeing according to your arrival time.
  • Spend some free time exploring the local surroundings.
  • Choose optional paragliding at an additional cost, subject to weather, operator availability and eligibility.
  • Return to your accommodation for the evening.
Overnight stay: Bir
DAY 02

Bir to Barot via Chehna Pass & Rajgundha

Bir → Chehna Pass → Rajgundha → Barot
  • Check out from your accommodation in Bir.
  • Begin your journey towards Barot via the proposed Chehna Pass and Rajgundha route.
  • Enjoy scenic stops along the way where access, conditions and the day's schedule permit.
  • Any required walking, trekking or guide arrangements will be agreed before the trip.
  • Arrive in Barot and check in to your selected accommodation.
  • Spend the evening at leisure.
Overnight stay: Barot
DAY 03

Barot to Joginder Nagar

Barot → Tikken → Jhatingri → Ghatasni → Joginder Nagar
  • Check out from your accommodation in Barot.
  • Depart towards Joginder Nagar via Tikken, Jhatingri and Ghatasni.
  • Enjoy suitable sightseeing or photo stops en route, depending on road conditions and your onward travel schedule.
  • Arrive in Joginder Nagar at your agreed drop-off point.
  • Continue with your onward journey.
Tour ends: Joginder Nagar
Sightseeing order, route and activities may change due to weather, road conditions, local restrictions and availability. Travel to Bir before Day 1 and onward travel after Day 3 are included only if stated in your quotation.

Frequently asked questions

Before You Travel.

Where does the tour start and finish?

The proposed itinerary starts in Bir on Day 1 and finishes in Joginder Nagar on Day 3. Exact pickup and drop-off arrangements are confirmed in your quotation.

Is paragliding included?

No. Paragliding is optional and costs extra unless explicitly included in your quotation. Participation is subject to operator requirements, weather and availability.

Is Day 2 entirely a vehicle journey?

This has not been confirmed. The proposed route via Chehna Pass and Rajgundha requires local verification. Any walking, trekking, transport or guide requirements must be explained and agreed before booking.

Can I customise the itinerary?

You can request different accommodation, additional nights or revised travel arrangements. Changes are subject to feasibility, availability and a revised quotation.

What are the payment and cancellation terms?

Request the applicable payment schedule, cancellation charges and refund conditions in writing before confirming your booking.
