Singapore City Escape

Discover the energy, culture and incredible attractions of Singapore during this 4-night, 5-day international holiday.

From the modern skyline of Marina Bay to beautiful gardens, colourful neighbourhoods and the entertainment of Sentosa Island, Singapore offers memorable experiences for travellers of all ages.

This package combines sightseeing with leisure time, allowing you to experience Singapore's famous attractions while still having time to explore the city at your own pace.

The itinerary can be customised according to your travel dates, preferred hotel category, flight schedule and selected attractions.

Tour Highlights

  • Explore Singapore's modern city highlights.
  • Discover the Marina Bay area.
  • Visit the spectacular Gardens by the Bay.
  • Experience the attractions of Sentosa Island.
  • See Singapore's famous skyline and landmarks.
  • Explore cultural neighbourhoods and shopping areas.
  • Opportunity to visit Universal Studios Singapore.
  • Enjoy free time for shopping and independent exploration.

Day-Wise Itinerary

DAY 1 • SINGAPORE

Arrival in Singapore

Welcome to Singapore. On arrival at Singapore Changi Airport, complete immigration and baggage formalities.

Meet your transfer representative and proceed to your hotel.

Complete hotel check-in and enjoy the remaining time at leisure.

Depending on your arrival time, you may explore nearby shopping areas, restaurants or enjoy an evening walk around the city.

Singapore stay • Arrival transfer
DAY 2 • CITY EXPERIENCE

Singapore City Tour

After breakfast, begin your Singapore sightseeing experience.

Discover some of the city's major landmarks and enjoy views of Singapore's impressive modern skyline.

Depending on the confirmed sightseeing programme, the tour may include popular areas such as Marina Bay, Merlion Park, Chinatown and other city highlights.

After the tour, enjoy leisure time for shopping, dining or independent exploration.

Singapore stay • Breakfast
DAY 3 • SENTOSA

Sentosa Island Experience

Enjoy breakfast at your hotel before preparing for a memorable Sentosa Island experience.

Discover Singapore's famous island entertainment district and enjoy the attractions included in your selected package.

Depending on your package, optional experiences can include Universal Studios Singapore and other Sentosa attractions.

Return to your hotel after your Sentosa experience.

Singapore stay • Breakfast
Universal Studios Singapore and other ticketed Sentosa attractions are included only when specifically mentioned in your final quotation.
DAY 4 • GARDENS & LEISURE

Gardens by the Bay & Free Time

After breakfast, continue exploring Singapore.

Visit the spectacular Gardens by the Bay area and enjoy its futuristic landscape.

Entry to selected conservatories and attractions can be arranged depending on your confirmed package.

Spend the remaining part of the day shopping or exploring Singapore at your own pace.

Singapore stay • Breakfast
Gardens by the Bay has both public areas and separately ticketed attractions. Your final voucher will specify which admissions are included.
DAY 5 • DEPARTURE

Singapore → Departure

Enjoy your final breakfast at the hotel before completing check-out formalities.

Depending on your flight schedule, enjoy some final leisure time before your airport transfer.

At the scheduled time, transfer to Singapore Changi Airport for your onward journey.

Leave Singapore with wonderful memories of your holiday.

Breakfast • Check-out • Airport transfer
