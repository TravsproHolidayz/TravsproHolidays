YOUR JOURNEY

Thailand, Day by Day.

Discover Thailand with this suggested 6-day itinerary. Enjoy city sightseeing, beautiful islands, cultural experiences and time to relax. The itinerary can be customised to suit your travel dates and preferences.

DAY 01 / ARRIVAL
✈️

Welcome to Thailand

Arrive in Bangkok or Phuket, depending on your selected package. Transfer to your hotel, complete check-in and enjoy some leisure time after your journey. Explore nearby markets or restaurants if time permits.

Airport Transfer Hotel Check-in Leisure Time
DAY 02 / CITY EXPLORATION
🌆

Discover Bangkok

Explore Bangkok's famous temples, cultural landmarks and lively shopping districts. Visit attractions such as Wat Arun or the Grand Palace, subject to opening hours and your selected sightseeing plan.

Temple Visits City Sightseeing Local Markets
DAY 03 / BEACH & ENTERTAINMENT
🏖️

Explore Pattaya

If your package includes Pattaya, travel to this popular coastal destination. Enjoy the beach atmosphere, explore Walking Street and discover local attractions at your own pace.

Beach Experience Local Attractions Evening Leisure
DAY 04 / ISLAND ADVENTURE
🚤

Coral Island Experience

Enjoy an optional excursion to Coral Island near Pattaya. Admire clear waters, relax on sandy beaches or explore available water activities according to local conditions.

Island Excursion Beach Time Water Activities
DAY 05 / CULTURE & SHOPPING
🛍️

Thai Culture & Shopping

Discover Thailand's local markets, street food and shopping destinations. Depending on your itinerary, enjoy free time for souvenirs, Thai cuisine, a traditional massage or a cultural attraction.

Shopping Thai Cuisine Local Experiences

Farewell to Thailand

Enjoy breakfast and some free time before checking out. Transfer to the airport for your return journey with wonderful memories of your Thailand holiday.
