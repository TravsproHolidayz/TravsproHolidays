Manali Mountain Holiday by Volvo

Enjoy a well-planned blend of mountain scenery, sightseeing and leisure time. Your holiday begins with an overnight Volvo journey from Delhi, followed by three hotel nights in Manali and an overnight return journey.

Explore Manali’s local landmarks, enjoy Solang Valley and discover the culture and heritage of Kullu and Naggar. Optional adventure activities are available at additional cost, subject to weather and applicable regulations.

The return coach departs Manali on Day 5. Delhi arrival is normally the following morning, subject to the coach schedule and road conditions.

Tour Highlights

  • Round-trip overnight Volvo journeys between Delhi and Manali.
  • Manali temples, Tibetan Monastery, Vashisht hot springs and Mall Road.
  • Solang Valley sightseeing and optional adventure activities.
  • Kullu culture, handicrafts and shawl showrooms.
  • Naggar Castle and Himalayan landscapes.
  • Nicholas Roerich Art Gallery and Tripura Sundari Temple.
  • Optional paragliding, rafting, zorbing, ATV rides and horse riding.

Day-Wise Itinerary

DAY 1 • DELHI

Delhi → Manali

Report at the designated Volvo boarding point in Delhi at the scheduled departure time. Board your coach and begin your overnight journey towards Manali.

Enjoy the journey as the landscape changes from the plains towards the Himalayan foothills.

Overnight coach journey • No hotel stay
DAY 2 • MANALI

Arrival & Local City Sightseeing

Arrive at the Manali Volvo stop and transfer to your hotel. Standard check-in is 12:00 noon. Early check-in is subject to availability and may involve additional charges.

Visit Hadimba Devi Temple, Ghatotkacha Temple, Club House, Tibetan Monastery, Vashisht Village and its hot springs. Explore the surrounding temples and enjoy evening leisure time at Mall Road.

Hotel night 1 • Dinner
Arrival-day breakfast is subject to the confirmed meal schedule and is not assumed to be included.
DAY 3 • SOLANG VALLEY

Manali → Solang Valley → Manali

After breakfast, proceed to Solang Valley and enjoy its mountain landscapes.

Optional paragliding, zorbing, ATV rides, horse riding and trail rides are available at your own expense, subject to weather and applicable regulations.

Return to Manali for dinner and your overnight hotel stay.

Hotel night 2 • Breakfast & dinner
DAY 4 • KULLU & NAGGAR

Kullu → Naggar → Manali

After breakfast, depart for Kullu and Naggar. En route, visit Devlok Cultural Theme Park and local shawl and handicraft showrooms.

Optional rafting and paragliding are available at additional cost. Continue to Naggar Castle and the Nicholas Roerich Art Gallery. Admire Tripura Sundari Temple en route and return to Manali in the evening.

Hotel night 3 • Breakfast & dinner
The supplied itinerary lists the art gallery as closed on Mondays. Visits depend on its operating schedule. Kullu–Manikaran may replace Kullu–Naggar if selected and confirmed.
DAY 5 • DEPARTURE

Manali → Delhi

After breakfast, check out according to hotel policy. Proceed towards the Mall Road area for leisure time. Cloakroom luggage storage is subject to availability and applicable terms.

A visit to Van Vihar may be possible depending on time. Collect your luggage and proceed to the Volvo boarding point for your overnight return journey to Delhi.

Breakfast • Check-out • Overnight coach
No hotel night is included on Day 5. Delhi arrival is normally the following morning.
