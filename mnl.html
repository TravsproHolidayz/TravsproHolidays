Your mountain escape

Manali Mountain Holiday by Volvo

Make your holiday from Delhi to Manali special with a well-planned blend of mountain scenery, sightseeing and leisure time. Start with an overnight Volvo journey and enjoy three hotel nights in Manali before your overnight return to Delhi.

Explore Manali’s local landmarks, experience Solang Valley and discover the culture, handicrafts and heritage of Kullu and Naggar. Optional adventure activities are available at additional cost, subject to weather and applicable regulations.

Duration: The itinerary runs from Day 1 to Day 5. The return coach departs Manali on Day 5, with Delhi arrival normally the following morning. Three hotel nights are included.
Holiday experiences

Tour Highlights

  • Comfortable overnight Volvo journeys between Delhi and Manali.
  • Manali temples, Tibetan Monastery, Vashisht hot springs and Mall Road.
  • Solang Valley sightseeing and optional adventure activities.
  • Kullu culture, handicrafts and shawl showrooms.
  • Naggar Castle and scenic Himalayan surroundings.
  • Nicholas Roerich Art Gallery and Tripura Sundari Temple.
  • Optional paragliding, river rafting, zorbing, ATV rides and horse riding.
Day by day

Your Manali Itinerary

DAY 01 DELHI • OVERNIGHT VOLVO Delhi → Manali

Report at the designated Volvo boarding point in Delhi at the scheduled departure time. Board your coach and begin your journey towards the mountains of Himachal Pradesh.

Enjoy the overnight journey as the landscape changes from the plains towards the Himalayan foothills.

Overnight coach journey • No hotel stay
DAY 02 MANALI • HOTEL NIGHT 1 Arrival & Local City Sightseeing

Arrive at the Manali Volvo stop, meet the representative and transfer to your hotel. Standard check-in time is 12:00 noon. Early check-in depends on availability and may cost extra.

After freshening up, visit Hadimba Devi Temple and nearby Ghatotkacha Temple. Continue to the Club House for optional indoor recreational activities.

Visit the Tibetan Monastery, Vashisht Village and its hot springs, along with the surrounding temples dedicated to Lord Shiva and Lord Rama. Enjoy evening leisure time at Mall Road for shopping and local food.

Hotel stay • Dinner under MAP plan
Arrival-day breakfast is subject to the confirmed meal schedule and is not assumed to be included.
DAY 03 MANALI • HOTEL NIGHT 2 Manali → Solang Valley → Manali

After breakfast, proceed to Solang Valley and enjoy its spectacular Himalayan landscapes.

Optional paragliding, zorbing, ATV rides, horse riding and trail rides are available at your own expense, subject to weather, operational availability and government regulations.

Return to Manali for dinner and your overnight hotel stay.

Hotel stay • Breakfast & dinner
DAY 04 MANALI • HOTEL NIGHT 3 Kullu → Naggar → Manali

After breakfast, depart for Kullu and Naggar. En route, visit Devlok Cultural Theme Park and local Kullu shawl and handicraft showrooms.

Optional river rafting and paragliding are available at additional cost, subject to weather and government regulations.

Continue to Naggar Castle, followed by the Nicholas Roerich Art Gallery. Admire Tripura Sundari Temple en route and return to Manali in the evening.

Hotel stay • Breakfast & dinner
The supplied itinerary lists the Roerich Art Gallery as closed on Mondays. Visits depend on its operating schedule. Kullu–Manikaran can replace Kullu–Naggar if selected and confirmed in your booking.
DAY 05 MANALI • OVERNIGHT RETURN VOLVO Leisure Time & Departure to Delhi

After breakfast, check out according to hotel policy and proceed by cab towards the Mall Road area. Cloakroom luggage storage is subject to availability and applicable terms.

Enjoy free time for shopping and leisure. A visit to Van Vihar may be possible depending on the available time.

Collect your luggage and proceed to the Volvo boarding point at the scheduled time. Board your pre-booked coach for the overnight return journey to Delhi.

Breakfast • Hotel check-out • Overnight coach
No hotel night is included on Day 5. Arrival in Delhi is normally the following morning, subject to the coach schedule.
