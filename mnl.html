Manali Mountain Holiday by Volvo

Enjoy a well-planned combination of mountain scenery, sightseeing and leisure time. Your holiday begins with an overnight Volvo journey from Delhi, followed by three hotel nights in Manali and an overnight return journey.

Explore Manali’s local landmarks, enjoy the landscapes of Solang Valley and discover the culture and heritage of Kullu and Naggar.

Optional adventure activities may be available at an additional cost, subject to weather, availability and applicable local regulations.

The return coach departs from Manali on Day 5. Arrival in Delhi is normally the following morning, subject to the coach schedule, weather and road conditions.

Tour Highlights

  • Round-trip overnight Volvo journeys between Delhi and Manali.
  • Manali temples, Tibetan Monastery, Vashisht hot springs and Mall Road.
  • Solang Valley sightseeing and optional adventure activities.
  • Kullu culture, handicrafts and traditional shawl showrooms.
  • Naggar Castle and beautiful Himalayan landscapes.
  • Nicholas Roerich Art Gallery and Tripura Sundari Temple.
  • Optional paragliding, rafting, zorbing, ATV rides and horse riding.

Day-Wise Itinerary

DAY 1 • DELHI

Delhi → Manali

Report at the designated Volvo boarding point in Delhi at the scheduled departure time. Board your coach and begin your overnight journey towards Manali.

Relax during the journey as the landscape changes from the plains towards the Himalayan foothills.

Overnight coach journey • No hotel stay
DAY 2 • MANALI

Arrival and Local City Sightseeing

Arrive at the Manali Volvo stop and transfer to your hotel. Standard check-in is at 12:00 noon. Early check-in is subject to hotel availability and may involve an additional charge.

Visit Hadimba Devi Temple, Ghatotkacha Temple, Club House, Tibetan Monastery, Vashisht Village and its hot springs. Explore the surrounding temples and enjoy leisure time at Mall Road in the evening.

Hotel night 1 • Dinner
Arrival-day breakfast is subject to the confirmed meal schedule and should not be considered included unless mentioned in your final booking confirmation.
DAY 3 • SOLANG VALLEY

Manali → Solang Valley → Manali

After breakfast, proceed to Solang Valley and enjoy its spectacular mountain landscapes.

Optional paragliding, zorbing, ATV rides, horse riding and trail rides may be available at your own expense, subject to weather and applicable regulations.

Return to Manali for dinner and your overnight hotel stay.

Hotel night 2 • Breakfast and dinner
DAY 4 • KULLU AND NAGGAR

Kullu → Naggar → Manali

After breakfast, depart for Kullu and Naggar. Along the way, visit Devlok Cultural Theme Park and local shawl and handicraft showrooms.

Optional rafting and paragliding may be available at an additional cost. Continue to Naggar Castle and the Nicholas Roerich Art Gallery.

Admire Tripura Sundari Temple along the route and return to Manali in the evening.

Hotel night 3 • Breakfast and dinner
The art gallery may be closed on Mondays. All visits remain subject to the attraction's current operating schedule. Kullu–Manikaran may replace Kullu–Naggar if selected and confirmed.
DAY 5 • DEPARTURE

Manali → Delhi

After breakfast, check out according to the hotel’s policy. Proceed towards the Mall Road area and enjoy leisure time.

Cloakroom luggage storage is subject to availability and applicable local terms. A visit to Van Vihar may be possible depending on the available time.

Collect your luggage and proceed to the Volvo boarding point for your overnight return journey to Delhi.

Breakfast • Check-out • Overnight coach
No hotel night is included on Day 5. Arrival in Delhi is normally the following morning.
