Royal Rajasthan Discovery

Experience the colours, history and remarkable landscapes of Rajasthan on this 5-night, 6-day journey through Jaipur, Jodhpur and Jaisalmer.

Walk through magnificent forts and palaces, discover bustling traditional markets and travel across changing landscapes towards Rajasthan's famous Thar Desert.

From the royal architecture of Jaipur to Jodhpur's historic landmarks and the golden surroundings of Jaisalmer, every destination reveals another side of Rajasthan.

Sightseeing schedules may change depending on monument operating hours, weather, road conditions and local operational requirements.

Tour Highlights

  • Explore Jaipur, Rajasthan's famous Pink City.
  • Visit the magnificent Amber Fort.
  • Discover Jaipur's City Palace and historic landmarks.
  • See the iconic Hawa Mahal.
  • Journey through Rajasthan's changing desert landscapes.
  • Explore the historic city of Jodhpur.
  • Visit the impressive Mehrangarh Fort.
  • Discover Jaisalmer Fort and the Golden City.
  • Experience the atmosphere of the Thar Desert.
  • Opportunity to enjoy a camel ride at the sand dunes.
  • Experience traditional Rajasthani culture.

Day-Wise Itinerary

DAY 1 • JAIPUR

Arrival in Jaipur

Welcome to Rajasthan. On arrival in Jaipur, meet your representative and proceed to your hotel.

Complete the check-in formalities and take some time to relax after your journey.

Depending on your arrival time, enjoy an evening exploring Jaipur's colourful local markets and traditional handicraft shops.

Jaipur stay • Overnight
DAY 2 • JAIPUR

Jaipur Royal Sightseeing

After breakfast, begin your full-day exploration of Jaipur.

Visit the magnificent Amber Fort and enjoy the impressive architecture and views from this historic complex.

Continue towards City Palace and explore Jaipur's royal heritage. See the iconic Hawa Mahal and visit other attractions included in your confirmed sightseeing plan.

In the evening, enjoy leisure time for local shopping before returning to your hotel.

Jaipur stay • Breakfast
DAY 3 • JODHPUR

Jaipur → Jodhpur

After breakfast, check out from your hotel and begin your drive towards Jodhpur.

Enjoy the changing Rajasthan landscape as you travel towards the famous Blue City.

On arrival, check into your hotel and relax. Depending on arrival time, enjoy leisure time to explore the surrounding market area.

Jodhpur stay • Breakfast
DAY 4 • JODHPUR / JAISALMER

Jodhpur Sightseeing → Jaisalmer

After breakfast, visit the magnificent Mehrangarh Fort and explore its impressive courtyards and historic surroundings.

Continue with other included Jodhpur attractions before beginning your journey towards Jaisalmer.

Arrive in the Golden City and check into your hotel. Relax for the evening and prepare for the next day's desert experience.

Jaisalmer stay • Breakfast
DAY 5 • JAISALMER

Jaisalmer City & Desert Experience

After breakfast, begin your sightseeing tour of Jaisalmer.

Explore Jaisalmer Fort and discover the historic streets, architecture and heritage of the Golden City.

Visit selected traditional havelis and other attractions included in your confirmed sightseeing programme.

Later, proceed towards the desert area to enjoy the spectacular sand-dune landscape.

Depending on your selected package, enjoy a camel ride, sunset views and a traditional Rajasthani cultural programme.

Jaisalmer / Desert stay • Breakfast
Camel rides, jeep safari, cultural programmes, desert-camp meals and other activities are included only when specifically mentioned in your final quotation.
DAY 6 • DEPARTURE

Jaisalmer Departure

Enjoy breakfast before completing your hotel or desert-camp check-out.

At the appropriate time, transfer to the designated railway station, airport or departure point according to your confirmed travel arrangements.

Your Rajasthan journey concludes with unforgettable memories of royal palaces, magnificent forts, colourful cities and the golden Thar Desert.
