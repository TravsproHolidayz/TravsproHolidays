Kashmir Paradise Holiday

Escape into the breathtaking landscapes of Kashmir on this thoughtfully planned 5-night, 6-day holiday.

From the lakes and Mughal gardens of Srinagar to the spectacular mountain scenery of Gulmarg and the peaceful valleys of Pahalgam, this package brings together some of Kashmir's most memorable experiences.

Enjoy comfortable accommodation, scenic road journeys, sightseeing and plenty of opportunities to experience the natural beauty of the region.

Sightseeing and excursions are subject to weather, road conditions, local authority regulations and operational conditions.

Tour Highlights

  • Explore Srinagar and its famous Mughal gardens.
  • Enjoy the beauty and mountain landscapes of Gulmarg.
  • Discover the picturesque valleys around Pahalgam.
  • Experience the atmosphere around Dal Lake.
  • Enjoy scenic drives through Kashmir's countryside.
  • Opportunity to experience a traditional Shikara ride.
  • Explore local markets and Kashmiri handicrafts.
  • Enjoy leisure time surrounded by Himalayan scenery.

Day-Wise Itinerary

DAY 1 • SRINAGAR

Arrival in Srinagar

Welcome to Kashmir. On arrival at Srinagar Airport, meet your representative and proceed to your hotel.

After check-in, relax and enjoy the beautiful surroundings of Srinagar.

Depending on your arrival time, you can explore the local market or enjoy leisure time around Dal Lake.

Srinagar stay • Dinner
DAY 2 • SRINAGAR

Srinagar Local Sightseeing

After breakfast, begin your sightseeing tour of Srinagar.

Explore the beautiful Mughal gardens, including Nishat Garden and Shalimar Garden, and enjoy panoramic views around Dal Lake.

Depending on the final itinerary, additional local attractions may also be included.

In the evening, you may enjoy a Shikara ride on Dal Lake if it is included in your confirmed package.

Srinagar stay • Breakfast and dinner
DAY 3 • GULMARG

Srinagar → Gulmarg → Srinagar

After breakfast, proceed towards Gulmarg for a memorable day surrounded by spectacular mountain scenery.

Enjoy leisure time in Gulmarg and explore the beautiful surroundings.

Guests may choose optional activities such as the Gondola ride, pony rides, skiing or other seasonal activities at an additional cost.

After sightseeing, return to Srinagar for dinner and your overnight stay.

Srinagar stay • Breakfast and dinner
Gondola tickets and optional activities should only be considered included when specifically stated in your final quotation.
DAY 4 • PAHALGAM

Srinagar → Pahalgam

After breakfast, check out and begin your scenic journey towards Pahalgam.

Travel through the beautiful Kashmir countryside and enjoy views of mountains, rivers and valleys along the way.

On arrival in Pahalgam, check into your hotel and enjoy leisure time exploring the surroundings.

Pahalgam stay • Breakfast and dinner
DAY 5 • PAHALGAM

Pahalgam Valley Experience

After breakfast, spend the day experiencing the natural beauty of Pahalgam.

Depending on your selected package and local transport regulations, you may explore popular nearby areas such as Betaab Valley, Aru Valley and Chandanwari.

Spend the remaining time enjoying the peaceful landscapes and mountain surroundings.

Pahalgam stay • Breakfast and dinner
Local sightseeing vehicles for certain Pahalgam attractions may need to be hired separately due to local transport regulations. Confirm the exact inclusions before booking.
DAY 6 • DEPARTURE

Pahalgam → Srinagar Airport

Enjoy breakfast at your hotel before checking out.

At the scheduled time, proceed towards Srinagar Airport for your onward journey.

Leave Kashmir with unforgettable memories of its mountains, valleys, lakes and beautiful landscapes.

Breakfast • Check-out • Airport transfer
Departure transfer timing will be planned according to your flight schedule, traffic and road conditions.
