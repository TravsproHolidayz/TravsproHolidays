MORE THAN MOUNTAIN VIEWS

The Shanan Story.
A Heritage Of Power.

Joginder Nagar's identity is closely connected with the development of hydroelectric power.

Shanan & The Uhl Connection

The Shanan hydroelectric project was commissioned in 1932. Its original generating capacity was 48 MW, later expanded to a total of 110 MW.


The project draws on the hydropower potential of the Barot area. Official tourism records describe water being tunnelled and piped from the Uhl River towards Joginder Nagar, where the Shanan power house was built by engineers headed by Colonel Batty.


This engineering history gives the town a distinctive story alongside its scenery, walking routes and nearby attractions.

Shanan project history: official PSPCL source ↗

Forests, Railways & Local Heritage

Joginder Nagar is the terminus of the narrow-gauge railway from Pathankot. A railway experience can be requested as an optional extension, subject to current services.


Nearby Jhatingri is described by Himachal tourism as a hilltop setting surrounded by deodar forest, with the ruins of the Mandi rulers' summer palace.


Machhyal, also spelt Macchiyal, is a small lake held sacred to Machendru Devta. Please respect local customs during your visit.

Joginder Nagar: official HPTDC destination guide ↗
Heritage access: This description does not include guaranteed entry into the Shanan power house, a trolley ride or access to operational railway or hydroelectric infrastructure. Any visit requires confirmation and applicable permission. Do not enter restricted sites or walk on railway tracks.
