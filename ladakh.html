YOUR DAY-BY-DAY JOURNEY

Six Days.
Your Ladakh Story.

Suggested overnight stays: Leh on Days 1, 2 and 5; Nubra Valley on Days 3 and 4.

DAY01

Arrival In Leh & Rest

Arrival · Hotel check-in · Acclimatisation
  • Arrive in Leh and transfer to your accommodation if included in your quotation.
  • Check in according to the hotel's scheduled check-in time.
  • Keep the day free for rest and acclimatisation.
  • No mountain-pass excursions or strenuous sightseeing are planned.
🏨 Overnight stay in Leh
DAY02

Leh Acclimatisation Day

Leh · Rest · Flexible leisure time
  • Continue your acclimatisation period in Leh.
  • Keep the schedule relaxed and avoid strenuous activity.
  • Any gentle nearby outing should depend on how you feel and local advice.
  • Confirm onward arrangements and applicable visitor documentation with your operator.
🏨 Overnight stay in Leh
DAY03

Leh To Nubra Valley

Leh → Khardung La → Nubra Valley
  • Depart only after completing at least 48 hours in Leh and if well enough to continue.
  • Follow the proposed route towards Nubra Valley via Khardung La, subject to road access.
  • Include suitable scenic stops according to conditions and the driver's advice.
  • Check in to your selected Nubra accommodation.
  • Keep the evening relaxed.
🏨 Overnight stay in Nubra Valley
DAY04

Nubra Valley Sightseeing

Suggested stops: Diskit · Hunder
  • Enjoy a flexible local sightseeing day based on your confirmed itinerary.
  • Request visits around Diskit and Hunder, subject to access and opening conditions.
  • Allow time for photographs and leisure.
  • Optional activities, entry fees and guide services are extra unless quoted.
  • Return to your accommodation for the evening.
🏨 Overnight stay in Nubra Valley
DAY05

Pangong Lake Visit & Return To Leh

Nubra Valley → Pangong Lake → Leh
  • Check out and start early according to your operator's confirmed schedule.
  • Travel towards Pangong Lake only if the route is open and the day's journey is feasible.
  • Enjoy a brief lake visit before continuing to Leh.
  • This is a long travel day. Stops and timing depend on road and weather conditions.
  • If the route is unsuitable, return to Leh using the operator's agreed alternative plan.
🏨 Overnight stay in Leh
DAY06

Departure From Leh

Hotel check-out · Onward journey
  • Check out according to your accommodation's policy.
  • Transfer to your agreed departure point if included.
  • Continue with your onward journey.
  • Ask about an extra buffer night if you prefer more flexibility.
📍 Tour ends in Leh
Before confirming: Your operator must verify route feasibility, seasonal accommodation, road access and applicable permits or visitor requirements for your nationality and travel dates. No pass, lake visit or activity is guaranteed. Additional nights may be needed if conditions change.

BEFORE YOU GO

A Few Helpful
Details.

Why are the first two days reserved for acclimatisation?

The official Leh advisory requires at least 48 hours of acclimatisation before travelling to higher-altitude areas. Departure timing must allow the full 48 hours.

Is an overnight stay at Pangong included?

No. This suggested itinerary proposes a Day 5 lake visit followed by a return to Leh. Request a longer itinerary if you want a different pace or an additional overnight stay.

Are all sightseeing visits guaranteed?

No. Roads, weather, access restrictions and your wellbeing may require changes. Your operator must confirm the route and alternatives before booking.

Are flights, meals and permits included?

Only if explicitly stated in your final quotation. Ask for a full breakdown of included and excluded services.

What are the cancellation terms?

Request the payment schedule, cancellation charges and refund conditions in writing before confirming your booking.
