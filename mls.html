YOUR JOURNEY

Malaysia, Day by Day.

Explore Malaysia with this suggested five-day itinerary. Experience Kuala Lumpur's landmarks, discover Genting Highlands and enjoy shopping, local cuisine and cultural attractions. The itinerary can be customised to match your preferred destinations and travel dates.

DAY 01 / ARRIVAL
✈️

Welcome to Malaysia

Arrive at Kuala Lumpur International Airport and transfer to your hotel. Complete check-in, relax after your journey and explore nearby restaurants or shopping areas if time permits.

Airport Transfer Hotel Check-in Leisure Time
DAY 02 / CITY EXPLORATION
🏙️

Discover Kuala Lumpur

Explore Malaysia's vibrant capital. Admire the Petronas Twin Towers, visit KLCC Park, discover Merdeka Square and enjoy the city's famous shopping and dining destinations.

Petronas Towers KLCC Park City Sightseeing
DAY 03 / HIGHLANDS
⛰️

Genting Highlands Adventure

Enjoy a trip to Genting Highlands for cooler mountain scenery, panoramic views and shopping. Consider a cable car ride or selected entertainment attractions, subject to availability.

Genting Highlands Cable Car Mountain Views
DAY 04 / CULTURE & SHOPPING
🛍️

Batu Caves & Local Experiences

Visit the colourful Batu Caves, discover Malaysian cultural heritage and explore local markets. Spend time shopping for souvenirs and tasting authentic Malaysian food.

Batu Caves Local Markets Malaysian Cuisine
DAY 05 / DEPARTURE
🌏

Farewell to Malaysia

Enjoy breakfast and some leisure time before checking out. Depending on your flight schedule, enjoy some last-minute shopping before your transfer to the airport for your return journey.

Breakfast Shopping Airport Transfer
